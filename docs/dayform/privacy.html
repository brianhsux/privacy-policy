日序 DayForm 隱私權政策

Brian Hsu · 2026-10-22

開發者與適用範圍

本政策適用於 Android 版日序 DayForm（app.dayform.reminder）。開發者為 Brian Hsu，隱私與使用問題請聯絡 brianhsudev@gmail.com。更新日期：2026-10-22。

資料保存在手機

任務名稱、日期、週期、分類、清單、待辦與操作紀錄儲存在 App 私有資料區。本版沒有帳號、家庭同步、廣告、分析追蹤或自動當機回報，也不會自動將任務或診斷上傳給開發者。

重開機與鎖定畫面

為在重開機首次解鎖前提醒，原生排程另將提醒名稱、時間、週期及響鈴設定儲存在裝置保護的 App 私有區域。名稱可能顯示於通知與鎖屏，並受系統鎖屏設定影響。

權限與音效

通知、精準鬧鐘、全螢幕提醒、前景播放、喚醒與震動權限用於提醒。自訂鈴聲由使用者在系統選擇器選取；不匯入或上傳音效。標題朗讀只選已安裝的離線 voice，不會主動要求線上朗讀。系統／語音服務本身的資料處理由其供應商管理。本 App 不要求聯絡人、位置、相機或麥克風權限。

備份與匯出

Android 自動備份與系統資料移轉已停用。你可主動匯出 JSON 備份或 CSV；檔案為未加密文字，包含任務與紀錄，不含音效檔。檔案由系統選擇器寫入你指定的位置；若選擇雲端供應商，將依該供應商的服務與政策處理。App 不自動傳送備份，請妥善保管已匯出的檔案。

問題回報與來信

問題回報只先儲存到你選定的位置，不自動寄送。你可選擇附上版本、權限、任務數量與排程狀態統計；診斷不含任務名稱、UUID、音效 URI 或裝置識別碼。你自行填寫的內容仍可能含個人資料。若你主動寄信給開發者，開發者會收到寄件地址、內容與你附加的檔案，僅用於處理詢問、排錯及回覆，不用於廣告或行銷。來信資料保留於處理詢問所需期間，可來信要求刪除。

保存與刪除

本機資料保留到你刪除、清除 App 儲存空間或解除安裝。刪除任務可復原，舊版遷移副本及短期診斷可能仍留在 App 內；若需完整清除，請清除 App 儲存空間或解除安裝，這也會停止提醒。本機診斷最多保留最近 100 筆事件。外部備份及回報須在儲存位置另外刪除。

網站與政策變更

公開隱私頁由 GitHub Pages 提供；瀏覽時 GitHub 可能依其政策處理 IP 位址等標準網頁請求資訊。這不是 DayForm 在背景上傳任務。若 App 的資料處理變更，將更新本政策與日期。隱私詢問：brianhsudev@gmail.com。

DayForm Privacy Policy

Brian Hsu · 2026-10-22

Developer and scope

This policy applies to DayForm for Android (app.dayform.reminder), developed by Brian Hsu. Contact: brianhsudev@gmail.com. Updated: 2026-10-22.

Local data

Reminder titles, dates, recurrence rules, categories, lists, pending occurrences and action history are stored in the app’s private storage. This release has no accounts, family sync, ads, analytics or automatic crash reporting. It does not automatically upload tasks or diagnostics to the developer.

Boot and lock screen

Reminder titles, schedules and sound settings are also stored in device-protected private storage to support reminders before the first unlock after reboot. Titles can appear on notifications and the lock screen, subject to system settings.

Permissions and audio

Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alerts, foreground audio, wake locks and vibration support reminders. You choose custom sounds through the system picker; the app does not upload audio. Title speech selects an installed offline voice and does not request online synthesis. System and speech providers manage their own services. The app does not request contacts, location, camera or microphone access.

Backup and export

Android automatic backup and system data transfer are disabled. You can export unencrypted JSON backups or CSV files containing tasks and history, but not sound files. The system picker writes to a destination you choose. A cloud storage provider may process a file under its own terms and policy. The app does not send backups automatically.

Reports and email

Reports are saved to your chosen location, not sent automatically. Optional diagnostics contain version, permissions and count/status statistics, not task titles, UUIDs, sound URIs or device identifiers. Your text may still contain personal information. If you email the developer, your sender address, message and attachments are received to investigate and answer your request, not for advertising. Correspondence is kept as needed to handle the inquiry; contact us to request deletion.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until deleted, app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Deleted tasks can be undone, and legacy migration copies and recent diagnostics can remain. Clear app storage or uninstall for full local removal; reminders will also stop. Local diagnostics retain up to 100 recent events. Delete exported files separately at their storage destination.

Website and changes

GitHub Pages hosts this policy. When you visit it, GitHub may process standard request information such as an IP address under its own policy; this is not background task uploading by DayForm. We will update this policy and date when data practices change. Contact: brianhsudev@gmail.com.
